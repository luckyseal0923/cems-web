MedKitVR 貴賓來訪

Last week we received a visit from Jason and Freek, cofounders of MedKitVR (www.medkitvr.com) who were part of a trade delegation from Holland. Their company offers several interesting and innovative services for VR in healthcare. We had a great time discussing VR use in health professions education. Thank you for your visit, we are looking forward to meeting you again in the near future!
